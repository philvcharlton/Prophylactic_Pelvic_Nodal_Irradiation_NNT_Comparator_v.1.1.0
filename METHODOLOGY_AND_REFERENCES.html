Prophylactic Pelvic Nodal Irradiation – NNT Comparator: Methodology and references

Calculation

For a desirable event-free outcome:

Absolute benefit = treatment rate − comparator rate
NNT = 1 ÷ absolute benefit expressed as a proportion

For an adverse-event rate, the subtraction is reversed so a reduction is treated as benefit. If the intervention increases the adverse outcome, the reciprocal is labelled NNH. Exact reciprocals are displayed to two decimal places; conventional clinical reporting rounds upward to the next whole patient.

Trial-derived values

  • POP-RT: five-year BFFS 95.0% versus 81.2%; DFS 89.5% versus 77.2%; DMFS 95.9% versus 89.2%.
  • SPPORT: five-year FFP 70.9% with PBRT, 81.3% with PBRT + short-term ADT, and 87.4% with PBRT + ADT + pelvic-node RT. Acute grade ≥2 adverse events were 19.7%, 37.7% and 44.6%; acute grade ≥3 events were 4.4%, 8.8% and 12.2% across the same arms.

The 100-patient tables treat each absolute percentage-point difference as one event per 100 patients and round to the nearest whole patient for communication. They are illustrations of trial-level averages, not predictions for an individual patient.

Technical notes

The package uses no third-party JavaScript libraries or external fonts. Trial constants are embedded in the HTML. Custom calculations occur locally in JavaScript. External reference links are optional and are the only functions requiring internet access.
