Prophylactic Pelvic Nodal Irradiation – NNT Comparator: Clinical disclaimer and intended use

Educational and research-use tool only. This comparator is not a medical device, prescribing system or validated clinical decision-support tool.

Limitations

NNT and NNH values are simple reciprocals of absolute differences between published rates at a stated timepoint. They do not incorporate confidence intervals, censoring, competing risks, baseline-risk heterogeneity, treatment interactions or individual patient characteristics. Kaplan–Meier estimates are treated as fixed percentages for communication purposes.

POP-RT and SPPORT enrolled different populations and used different treatment strategies, endpoints, follow-up periods and toxicity scales. Their estimates must not be compared as though generated by one trial. Descriptive NNH values based on non-significant differences do not establish treatment-related harm.

Clinical responsibility

Use requires independent review of the original publications, current guidelines, eligibility, imaging, comorbidity, expected toxicity, competing risks and patient preferences. No patient-identifiable information should be entered.

Privacy

The package performs calculations locally in the browser. It contains no backend, analytics or data transmission.
