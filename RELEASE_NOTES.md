# Release notes

## Standalone v1.3.0 — 28 September 2026

- Renamed the product consistently to “Prophylactic Pelvic Nodal Irradiation – NNT Comparator”.
- Updated the browser title, visible heading, footer, installable-app name, validation report and all supporting documents.
- Preserved the existing calculation engine, trial data, toxicity/QOL summaries and 100-patient trade-off tables unchanged.

## Standalone v1.2.0 — 26 September 2026

- Added the SPPORT “clinical trade-off in 100 comparable salvage patients” table.
- Separates adding short-term ADT to PBRT, adding pelvic-node RT to PBRT + ADT, and the full strategy versus PBRT alone.
- Reports approximate changes in five-year FFP events, acute grade ≥2 and ≥3 adverse events, late GI/GU and blood/bone-marrow toxicity, QOL and overall survival.
- Adds a horizontally scrollable mobile layout for the four-column comparison.
- Preserves the warning that endpoints overlap and must not be summed.

## Standalone v1.1.0 — 23 September 2026

- Added “The clinical trade-off in 100 comparable patients” POP-RT table.
- Shows approximately 14 fewer biochemical failures, 12 fewer DFS events and 7 fewer distant-metastasis/DMFS events per 100 comparable patients receiving whole-pelvic rather than prostate-only RT.
- Separates statistically supported benefits and urinary toxicity from outcomes with no proven between-group difference.
- Adds an explicit warning that outcome categories overlap and must not be summed.

## Standalone v1.0.0 — 18 September 2026

- Initial offline package derived from the published Prophylactic Pelvic Nodal Irradiation – NNT Comparator.
- Includes POP-RT efficacy, late toxicity and QOL context.
- Includes SPPORT efficacy, acute and late toxicity, and the 2026 patient-reported QOL analysis.
- Shows unrounded NNT/NNH to two decimal places and conventional whole-patient rounding.
- Shows the underlying absolute-difference subtraction below each trial result.
- Adds custom event-free outcome and adverse-event calculations.
- Adds browser-only privacy statement, mobile/PWA metadata, subfolder-safe service worker and offline cache.
- Adds automated known-answer validation, user guide, methodology/references and clinical disclaimer.

### Evidence snapshot

Evidence and links were checked in September 2026. Trial estimates are intentionally fixed to preserve reproducibility; review current guidance and later publications before clinical use.
